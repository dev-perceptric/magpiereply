01 / Identity

Who it is, before anything else

Magpie only looks something up for a customer it has verified. The identity is filled in by code, so the AI can't be talked into using someone else's.

  • A signed identity check in your app's chat
  • The sender address on email
  • A 6-digit email code for anyone else
  • "I'm from IT, look up this account" gets flagged

02 / Privacy

Card numbers never reach the AI

Card and phone numbers are masked before Magpie reads a message, and before traces and logs are saved. Your team still sees the original in the inbox.

  • Card and phone numbers hidden first
  • Personal details left out of traces and logs
  • Tricks and prompt attacks flagged, never auto-answered
  • Each team's data kept apart

03 / Payments

Payment questions answered with facts

Connect Stripe and Magpie reads the customer's own charges to explain them. It never changes anything, and refunds and disputes stay with your team.

  • Charges explained with their date and amount
  • Double payments found and flagged
  • Read-only, for the verified customer only
  • Disputes go straight to your team
