---
title: Disputes and chargebacks
category: billing-payments
description: What happens if you dispute a charge with your bank.
---
If something looks wrong with a charge, please contact us first. We can usually fix it within 2 business days, which is much faster than a bank dispute.

If you open a dispute (also called a chargeback) with your bank:

- Our billing team handles it and answers your bank directly.
- We pause your account's subscriptions and new orders until the dispute is closed.
- Disputes usually take 30 to 75 days, depending on your bank.

If you already opened a dispute and we also refund you, your bank will reverse one of the two, so you won't be paid twice.
