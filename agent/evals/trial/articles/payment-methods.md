---
title: Payment methods we accept
category: billing-payments
description: Cards, wallets and gift cards you can pay with.
---
We accept:

- Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- Tidewell gift cards

We don't accept PayPal, cryptocurrency or cash on delivery.

All prices are in US dollars. If your card is from outside the US, your bank converts the amount and may charge a foreign transaction fee.

Tidewell gift cards never expire and can be used on any order or subscription. They can't be exchanged for cash.
