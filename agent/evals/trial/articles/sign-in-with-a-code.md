---
title: Signing in to your account
category: account
description: How sign-in codes work and what to do if you don't get one.
---
We don't use passwords. To sign in:

1. Go to our website and click **Sign in**.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter the 6-digit code we email you. Codes expire after **10 minutes**.

**No code?** Check your spam folder, wait 2 minutes, then click **Send a new code**.

**Lost access to your email?** Contact us. To protect your account, we'll ask for a recent order number and the last 4 digits of the card on the account before we make any change.
