---
title: When will I get my refund?
category: returns-refunds
description: How long refunds take to reach your card or bank.
---
Once we approve a refund (a canceled order, a return that arrived or a duplicate charge), we issue it within **2 business days**.

After we issue it, your bank takes **5 to 10 business days** to show it on your statement. We can't speed up this part, because it depends on your bank.

- Refunds always go back to the original payment method.
- We email you when the refund is issued, with a reference number. If it hasn't shown up after 10 business days, give that number to your bank.
- Orders paid with a Tidewell gift card are refunded to the gift card right away.
