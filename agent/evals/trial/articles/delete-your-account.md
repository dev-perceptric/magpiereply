---
title: Delete your account and data
category: account
description: Close your account and remove your personal data.
---
1. Sign in and go to **Account → Profile**.
2. Click **Delete account** and confirm.

This cancels any subscriptions and removes your saved cards and addresses. We delete your personal data within **30 days**.

We keep order records that tax law requires for 7 years, with no marketing use. Any gift card balance is lost when the account is deleted, so use it first.
