---
title: Sales tax
category: billing-payments
description: When we charge sales tax and VAT.
---
**United States:** we charge sales tax on orders shipped to states that require it. The amount is based on your shipping address and shows at checkout.

**United Kingdom:** we collect VAT at checkout on orders of £135 or less.

**Canada:** we don't collect Canadian taxes at checkout. See **Customs, duties and import taxes**.

If you're tax-exempt in the US, contact us with your exemption certificate before you order.
