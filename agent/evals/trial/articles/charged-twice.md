---
title: I was charged twice
category: billing-payments
description: How to tell a duplicate charge from a pending hold, and how we fix it.
---
First, check your bank's app to see if one of the charges is **pending**.

- **One charge is pending:** it's usually a hold, and it drops off by itself within 3 to 7 business days. You won't pay it. See **Pending charges and card holds**.
- **Both charges are posted (completed)** for the same amount and day: this may be a duplicate.

For a duplicate, contact us with:

- the date and amount of both charges
- the last 4 digits of the card

We'll check our records. If we find a duplicate, we refund it, and you'll get an email when the refund is issued. Your bank then takes 5 to 10 business days to show it.

Two charges on different days are usually two separate orders or a subscription renewal. Check **Account → Orders** to match each charge to an order.
