---
title: Tracking says delivered, but I don't have it
category: orders-shipping
description: What to do when a package shows as delivered but didn't arrive.
---
Packages marked delivered sometimes show up a day or two later. First:

1. Wait 48 hours after the delivery scan.
2. Check around your door, your mailroom and with neighbors.
3. Check that the address on the order is right.

Still missing? Contact us within **14 days** of the delivery date with your order number. We'll reship the order once, at no cost, or refund it if you prefer. For a second missing package to the same address, we'll ask you to file a claim with the carrier first.
