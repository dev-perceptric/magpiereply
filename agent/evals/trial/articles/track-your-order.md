title: Track your order
category: orders-shipping
description: Find your tracking link and what each tracking status means.
---
When your order ships, we email you a tracking link. You can also find it any time:

1. Sign in and go to **Account → Orders**.
2. Open the order and click **Track package**.

We use USPS and UPS in the US, Canada Post in Canada and Royal Mail in the UK.

Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan after we email you. If tracking hasn't moved for 5 business days in the US, or 10 business days for Canada or the UK, contact us and we'll check with the carrier.
