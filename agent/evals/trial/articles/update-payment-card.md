---
title: Update the card on your account
category: billing-payments
description: Add a new card or change the card used for subscriptions.
---
1. Sign in and go to **Account → Billing → Payment methods**.
2. Click **Add card** and enter the details.
3. Click **Make default** next to the new card.

Subscriptions always use your default card. When you add a card, we place a $1 hold to check it. The hold drops off within a few days.

To remove an old card, click **Remove** next to it. You can't remove your default card while you have an active subscription.
