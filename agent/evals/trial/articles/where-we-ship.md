We ship from our roastery in Portland, Oregon to the **United States, Canada and the United Kingdom**. We don't ship to other countries yet.

| Where | Option | Price | Delivery time after it ships |
|---|---|---|---|
| United States | Standard | $6, free on orders of $50 or more | 3 to 5 business days |
| United States | Express | $15 | 1 to 2 business days |
| Canada | Standard (Canada Post) | $14 | 5 to 10 business days |
| United Kingdom | Standard (Royal Mail) | $18 | 7 to 12 business days |

- The $50 free shipping amount is counted after discounts and before tax. It applies to US orders only.
- Express isn't available to PO boxes or APO/FPO addresses.
- Subscription shipments ship free within the US.
- Orders to Canada and the UK may owe import taxes. See **Customs, duties and import taxes**.
