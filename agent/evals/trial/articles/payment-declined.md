---
title: My payment was declined
category: billing-payments
description: Common reasons a card is declined and what to try.
---
Check that:

- the card number, expiry date and security code are right
- the billing ZIP or postal code matches what your bank has on file
- the card has enough available credit

Cards from Canada and the UK are sometimes blocked by the bank because the payment is from a US store. Call your bank to allow it, or try Apple Pay or Google Pay.

**Subscriptions:** if a renewal payment is declined, we email you and try again after 1 day and 3 days. If it still fails, we pause the subscription until you update your card in **Account → Billing**.
