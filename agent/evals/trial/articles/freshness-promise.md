---
title: Our Freshness promise
category: returns-refunds
description: Don't love a coffee? We'll replace it.
---
If you don't love a coffee, tell us within **30 days of delivery** and we'll send you a different coffee of the same size, or give you store credit for it.

- One bag per order is covered.
- You don't need to send the coffee back.
- For subscriptions, it covers one bag per shipment.

Just contact us with your order number and what you didn't like (too bitter, too light, not fresh). We use your notes to suggest a better match.
