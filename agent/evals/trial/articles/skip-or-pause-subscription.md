---
title: Skip or pause your subscription
category: subscriptions
description: Take a break without canceling.
---
**Skip one shipment:** go to **Account → Subscriptions** and click **Skip next shipment**. Your following shipment goes out on schedule.

**Pause:** click **Pause** and pick a length from 2 weeks to 3 months. Your subscription restarts on its own when the pause ends, and we email you 3 days before.

Skips and pauses must be made before 11:59 pm Pacific time the day before your renewal date to avoid that charge.
