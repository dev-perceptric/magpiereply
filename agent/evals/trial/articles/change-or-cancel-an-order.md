---
title: Change or cancel an order
category: orders-shipping
description: How to edit or cancel an order after you place it.
---
You can change or cancel an order yourself for **1 hour** after you place it:

1. Go to **Account → Orders** and open the order.
2. Click **Edit order** to change items or the address, or **Cancel order** to cancel it.

If you cancel, the full amount goes back to your card. See **When will I get my refund?** for timing.

After 1 hour, contact us. If your order is still marked **Received**, we can usually change or cancel it. Once it's **Roasting**, we can't change it, but you can return unused brewing gear after it arrives.
