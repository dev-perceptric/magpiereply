---
title: Start a return
category: returns-refunds
description: Step-by-step instructions to send gear back.
---
1. Sign in and go to **Account → Orders**.
2. Open the order and click **Return items**.
3. Pick the items and a reason.
4. **US orders:** print the free prepaid label we email you. **Canada and UK orders:** ship the items back to us with any tracked service. Return shipping is at your cost.

Send returns to:
Tidewell Coffee Returns, 1800 NW Marlow Ave, Portland, OR 97209, USA

Pack the gear in its original box. We'll email you when the return arrives and again when the refund is issued.
