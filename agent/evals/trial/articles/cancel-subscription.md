---
title: Cancel your subscription
category: subscriptions
description: How to cancel, and what happens to a shipment that's already charged.
---
1. Go to **Account → Subscriptions**.
2. Click **Cancel subscription** and follow the steps.

Cancel before **11:59 pm Pacific time the day before** your renewal date and you won't be charged again.

If you've already been charged for a renewal, contact us right away. If that shipment hasn't started roasting, we'll cancel it and refund you. Once it's roasting, it will ship as normal.

You can restart a canceled subscription any time from the same page.
