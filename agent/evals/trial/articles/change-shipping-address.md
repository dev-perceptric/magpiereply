---
title: Change the shipping address on an order
category: orders-shipping
description: Fix an address before or after your order ships.
---
**Within 1 hour of ordering:** open the order in **Account → Orders** and click **Edit order**.

**After 1 hour, before it ships:** contact us with your order number and the new address. We can change it until the order is marked **Shipped**.

**After it ships:** we can't change the address. Use the tracking link to ask the carrier to hold or redirect the package. If the package comes back to us, we'll reship it to the right address for the cost of shipping.

To change the address for future subscription shipments, see **Change your subscription**.
