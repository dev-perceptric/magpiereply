---
title: Change your subscription
category: subscriptions
description: Change the coffee, bag size, schedule, address or card.
---
Go to **Account → Subscriptions** and click **Edit** on the subscription. You can change:

- the coffee, or choose **Roaster's pick** for a new coffee each time
- the bag size (12 oz or 2 lb)
- how often it ships (every 1, 2 or 4 weeks)
- the shipping address
- the card (it uses your default card; see **Update the card on your account**)

Changes made before 11:59 pm Pacific time the day before your renewal date apply to your next shipment.
