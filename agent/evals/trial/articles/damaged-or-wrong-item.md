---
title: My order arrived damaged or wrong
category: orders-shipping
description: Report a damaged, leaking or wrong item.
---
Sorry about that. Contact us within **14 days** of delivery with:

- your order number
- a photo of the item and the box

We'll send a replacement at no cost. You don't need to send the damaged or wrong item back.

If the item you need is out of stock, we'll refund it to your original payment method instead.
