---
title: How subscriptions work
category: subscriptions
description: Shipment schedules, renewal charges and reminders.
---
A subscription sends you fresh coffee every **1, 2 or 4 weeks**.

- Bags come in 12 oz or 2 lb.
- Subscriptions ship free within the US.
- We charge your default card on your **renewal date**, around 6:00 am Pacific time, and roast your coffee on the next roast day.
- We email you a reminder **3 days before** each renewal.

To make changes for the next shipment, make them before **11:59 pm Pacific time the day before** your renewal date. Changes after that apply to the shipment after next.

Manage everything in **Account → Subscriptions**.
