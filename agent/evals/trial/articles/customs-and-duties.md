---
title: Customs, duties and import taxes
category: orders-shipping
description: Import taxes for orders shipped to Canada and the UK.
---
**Canada:** We don't collect Canadian taxes or duties at checkout. Canada Post may ask you to pay GST/HST, duties and a handling fee before delivery. These fees aren't refundable by us.

**United Kingdom:** For orders of £135 or less, we collect UK VAT at checkout, so there's nothing more to pay on delivery. Orders over £135 may owe VAT and a handling fee to Royal Mail.

If you refuse a package because of import fees, it comes back to us. We refund the items, minus the original shipping cost, once it arrives.
