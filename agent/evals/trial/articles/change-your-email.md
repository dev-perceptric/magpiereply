---
title: Change your email address
category: account
description: Update the email you sign in with.
---
1. Sign in and go to **Account → Profile**.
2. Next to **Email**, click **Change**.
3. Enter your new email address.
4. We send a 6-digit code to the new address. Enter it to confirm.

Your orders, subscriptions and gift card balance stay with your account. We also send a notice to your old address, in case you didn't make the change.

If you can't sign in to make the change, see **Signing in to your account**.
