---
title: Pending charges and card holds
category: billing-payments
description: Why you might see a pending charge you didn't expect.
---
A pending charge is a hold your bank places while a payment is checked. Common reasons:

- **You added a new card.** We place a $1 hold to check the card. It's never charged.
- **A payment failed and you tried again.** The failed try can show as pending.
- **You edited or canceled an order.** The first amount can stay pending for a few days.

Holds drop off by themselves within **3 to 7 business days**, depending on your bank. We can't remove a hold from our side; only your bank can release it early.

If a charge moves from pending to posted and you don't recognize it, see **I was charged twice**.
