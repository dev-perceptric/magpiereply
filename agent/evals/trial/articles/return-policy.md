---
title: Our return policy
category: returns-refunds
description: What you can return, and for how long.
---
**Brewing gear:** You can return unused gear in its original packaging within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund of the item price.

**Coffee:** We can't take back opened coffee. If you don't love a coffee, our **Freshness promise** covers you.

**Gift cards** can't be returned or refunded.

Shipping costs aren't refunded, unless the return is because of our mistake (damaged, wrong or missing items).

To start, see **Start a return**.
