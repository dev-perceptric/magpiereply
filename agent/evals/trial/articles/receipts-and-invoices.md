---
title: Receipts and invoices
category: billing-payments
description: Download a receipt or add business details to invoices.
---
**Download a receipt:** go to **Account → Orders**, open the order and click **Download receipt**. You get a PDF with the items, tax and payment method.

**Add a business name or VAT number:** go to **Account → Billing → Billing details** before you order. Receipts for new orders will show them. For an order you've already placed, contact us and we'll send an updated receipt.

Subscription receipts are emailed after each renewal.
