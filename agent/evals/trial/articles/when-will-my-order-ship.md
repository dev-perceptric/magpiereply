---
title: When will my order ship?
category: orders-shipping
description: Our roast days, the daily cutoff and how long it takes to ship.
---
We roast every order fresh, Monday through Thursday.

- Orders placed **before 12:00 pm Pacific time on a roast day** are roasted that day and ship the next business day.
- Orders placed after the cutoff, or on Friday, Saturday or Sunday, are roasted on the next roast day.
- Orders with brewing gear only (no coffee) ship within 1 business day.

Your order page shows where your order is: **Received**, **Roasting**, **Packed** or **Shipped**. We email you a tracking link as soon as it ships.

On US holidays we don't roast or ship. Orders placed then go out on the next roast day.
