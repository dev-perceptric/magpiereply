*Last updated: February 2025*

Good news: all US orders of **$35 or more** now ship free with standard shipping!

Orders under $35 pay a flat **$5** for standard shipping. Standard orders ship with FedEx Ground Economy and arrive in 4 to 7 business days.

This offer applies to one-time orders and subscriptions shipped within the contiguous US.
