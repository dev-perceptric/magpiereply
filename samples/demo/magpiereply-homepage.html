Getting started
What is MagpieReply?

A shared support inbox for chat, email and social messages, with Magpie, an AI agent that writes replies from your own help content.

How do I set it up?

Sign up, add your chat widget or support email, then turn Magpie on in Settings and pick its inboxes.

Do I need Magpie to use the inbox?

No. The inbox works on its own. Turn Magpie on when you're ready.

Pricing and limits
Is it free?

Yes, while we're in early access. Each team has a monthly AI spending limit.

What happens at the limit?

Magpie stops calling the AI for the rest of the month, and conversations go to your team.

Safety and data
What if Magpie gets it wrong?

In Draft mode a teammate reads every reply first, and the fact check flags any claim it can't find in a source.

Can Magpie see other customers' data?

No. Lookups use the signed-in customer's email, filled in by code.

Can Magpie change anything in my apps?

No. It only uses read-only tools. Refunds and changes stay with your team.
