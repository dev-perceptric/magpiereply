Built so you can trust it

Magpie only says what your sources say. When it isn't sure, it hands the chat to a person.

Every reply fact checked

Each claim is matched to your sources before it goes out. Replies that fail never reach the customer.

Private data hidden

Card numbers and other private details are masked before the AI ever sees them.

Tricks blocked

Messages that try to make Magpie ignore its rules are caught and turned down.

Caps and a kill switch

Set a monthly spending cap. Pause Magpie for every inbox with one click.

Only their own records

When Magpie looks something up in Stripe or Linear, it only sees what belongs to that customer.

Clean handoffs

When a person takes over, Magpie leaves a short note so nobody asks the customer twice.
