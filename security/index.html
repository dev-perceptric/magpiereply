Built so a wrong step is hard to take

These rules are part of how Magpie works, on every plan.

Identity first

Magpie only looks things up for a customer it has verified: a signed identity check in chat, the sender on email, or a one-time code. Identity is filled in by code, never typed by the AI.

Read-only apps

Magpie only gets the tools an app marks as read-only. Tools that create refunds or change records are never offered to it.

Private data hidden

Names, emails, card numbers and other personal details are masked before the AI reads a message and before traces and logs are saved.

Screening for prompt attacks

Every customer message is checked for attempts to trick Magpie. Flagged messages never get an automatic reply.

Keys stored encrypted

Sign-ins for your apps and Magpie's own keys are stored encrypted, and refreshed before they expire.

Teams kept apart

Each team's data is kept separate on every row, so one team's Magpie only sees its own conversations, knowledge and apps.

You stay in control

See everything and stop anything

Kill switches

Turn Magpie off for one inbox or your whole team, in one click.

Explain any reply

Traces show every step behind a reply: the sources it read, the lookups it made and the checks it passed.

Spending caps

Set a monthly limit. When it's reached, Magpie hands new conversations to your team.

Tests before going live

Replay your past conversations against Magpie before it talks to a real customer.

Draft mode

Start with Magpie writing drafts that your team sends. Switch to Auto when you trust it.

Self-host the inbox

Run the open source inbox on your own servers to keep conversations on your own hardware.

Coming next

Actions, with approvals

When Magpie learns to take actions, like a refund, they will come with the same care: money and account changes wait for a person on your team, every action has amount limits and daily caps, and every step is logged on the conversation.
