03 / CHECKED

Every reply is checked before anyone sees it

A second model reads each reply against the articles and lookups it used. It also watches for customers trying to trick Magpie.

  • Sources listed on every reply
  • Claims it can't find in a source are flagged by name
  • "Ignore your rules" or "I'm from IT" gets flagged, and never gets an automatic reply
  • Card and phone numbers are hidden before the AI reads a message

04 / DRAFT AND AUTO

Your team sends first. Magpie earns Auto.

In Draft mode, Magpie's reply waits in a side panel with Send, Edit and Discard. A replay test answers up to 30 past conversations again and grades each one against what your team sent.

  • Auto unlocks after a replay test of at least 10 replies, with 90% passing
  • In Auto, refunds, flagged messages and handoffs still wait for a person
  • After two answers, a customer who still needs help goes to your team
  • Off, Draft or Auto, chosen for each inbox

Questions about Magpie

What happens if Magpie gets something wrong?

In Draft mode, a teammate reads every reply before it goes out. The fact check flags any claim it can't find in a source, so your team knows where to look.

Auto only unlocks once a replay test shows Magpie answering past conversations the way your team did, at least 9 times in 10.

What does it cost?

Magpie is free while we're in early access. Each team has a monthly spending limit on AI, so there are no surprise bills. See pricing for the plans we're planning.

Can Magpie see other customers' data?

No. Lookups use the signed-in customer's email, filled in by code, so Magpie can only look up the person it's talking to. It never looks up anyone else, even for someone claiming to be staff.

Which apps can Magpie use?

Stripe and Linear today, each with one click. HubSpot and Shopify are next. Magpie only reads from your apps.

Can I turn Magpie off?

Any time. Settings, AI agent has Off, Draft and Auto, and you pick which inboxes Magpie works in.
