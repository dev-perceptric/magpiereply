How a reply happens

Every reply follows your knowledge and your rules

A customer writes in. The agent finds the facts, checks what it's allowed to do, then answers or passes the ticket on. You can see every step it took.

01 / KNOWLEDGE

Answers from your docs, with the source attached

Point it at your help center, your website, PDFs and solved tickets. It keeps up as they change.

  • Searches by meaning and by keyword, then keeps the best few pieces
  • Shows where each answer came from
  • Checks every claim against a source before it sends

02 / PLAYBOOKS AND RULES

Write how you handle a case in plain English

A playbook is a set of steps for one kind of request, like a double charge or a lost package. Rules are short policies that apply when they fit.

  • Your playbook is turned into steps and checked for gaps before it runs
  • Rules like "never promise a ship date" apply only when they fit the moment
  • Fixed wording for sensitive moments, like refunds or outages
  • Versions with Draft, Testing and Live status

04 / HANDOFF

Passes the hard ones to your team with a summary

The agent hands off when a customer asks for a person or seems upset. It also hands off when the answer is missing, after two failed tries, or when a fact check fails.

  • A private note sums up the thread and what was tried
  • The right team is assigned and the customer is told
  • An out-of-office reply after hours

Questions

Common questions

Does it make things up?

It answers only from your knowledge and your connected apps. Before a reply goes out, a fact check confirms each claim has a source. If the check fails, the ticket goes to your team.

Can it issue refunds?

Yes, if you allow it. Most teams set refunds to need a person's approval, with an amount limit and a daily cap. Each refund is logged on the conversation, and a retry can never run it twice.

What happens when it doesn't know the answer?

It hands the ticket to the right team with a short note about what the customer needs and what was tried. The customer is told a person will reply.

Which AI model does it use?

Claude, by Anthropic. Quick tasks like sorting and checks use a small, fast model. Replies use a larger one, and hard investigations use the most capable one.

Can I try it on past tickets first?

Yes. Replay tests run your past conversations through the agent and show how many it would solve, hand off or get wrong. You can also start in Draft mode, where your team sends every reply.
