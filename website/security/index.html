How the agent stays safe

Built so a wrong step is hard to take

These rules are part of how the agent works. They apply on every plan.

01

Identity first

The agent only looks up or changes things for a customer it has verified.

  • A signed identity check in the website chat
  • The sender address on email
  • A one-time code by email before anything sensitive
  • Identity fields are filled in by code. The model never types them.
02

Risk levels and approvals

You decide what the agent may do on its own.

  • Safe actions run on their own
  • Some actions wait for the customer to confirm in the chat
  • Money and account changes wait for a person on your team to approve
03

Limits on every action

Even an approved action has guardrails.

  • Amount limits, such as refunds up to $50
  • Daily caps per action
  • A unique key on every action, so a retry can't run it twice
  • Every action is logged on the conversation
04

Personal data stays out of logs

Names, emails, card numbers and other personal details are masked before traces and logs are saved.

05

Screening for prompt attacks

Every customer message is checked for attempts to trick the agent before it is read.

06

Keys stored encrypted

Sign-ins for your apps and the agent's own keys are stored encrypted. Our team can't read them in the database.

07

Teams kept apart

Each team's data is kept separate on every row, so one team's agent can only see its own conversations, knowledge and apps.

Questions

Security questions

Do you train AI models on our data?

We don't use your conversations to train AI models. The agent uses Claude through Anthropic's API, and Anthropic's commercial terms say API inputs aren't used to train their models.

Where is our data stored?

MagpieReply Cloud runs on managed Postgres. If you self-host, everything stays on your own servers.

Can we delete our data?

Yes. Delete your account in the app, or email us and we'll delete it for you.

Can we read the code?

Yes. The inbox is open source under the MIT license. Read it on GitHub.
