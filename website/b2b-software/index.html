01 / Docs

How-to answers from your docs

Magpie reads your help center, docs site and files, and answers setup and how-to questions with the article it used.

  • Docs sites up to 50 pages, read again every day
  • Help center articles, read again every 15 minutes
  • PDF, Word and Markdown files up to 20 MB
  • Every answer lists the article behind it

02 / Known bugs

Tell customers when a bug is fixed

When a customer reports a problem, Magpie checks Linear for a matching issue and tells them its status in plain words.

  • Whether the bug is known, or already fixed
  • Searches only your own team's issues
  • No issue links, ids or teammate names shared
  • New problems go to your team with a note
Looked up for this replyTrace
Searched "CSV export fails"In progress
MagpieThanks for flagging it. This is a known bug with exports over 10,000 rows, and the fix is planned for this week. Exporting one month at a time works until then.
03 / Billing

Charge questions, answered from the account itself

Magpie checks the signed-in customer's recent charges in your Stripe account and spots double payments. Refunds and disputes stay with your team.

  • Each charge explained with its date and amount
  • Double payments found and flagged
  • The customer's email is filled in by code, so Magpie can't look up anyone else
  • Bank disputes go straight to Billing

04 / The right team

The hard ones reach the right person

Rules send billing, security and bug reports to the right team. When Magpie hands off, it leaves a note so nobody asks the customer twice.

  • Rules assign by keyword, inbox or custom field like plan
  • Teams like Billing, with new chats handed out in turn
  • Handoff notes that sum up the problem
  • Private notes and @mentions inside the thread
