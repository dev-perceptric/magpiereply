Where Zendesk is ahead today

We'd rather you hear it from us.

A long track record

Zendesk has run help desks for big teams for years, with a deep feature list.

Quality assurance

Zendesk sells AI quality checks for your team's replies. MagpieReply checks Magpie's replies only.

A huge app marketplace

Zendesk connects to a long list of apps. MagpieReply reads Stripe and Linear today, with more on the way.

Switching

Move over in an afternoon

1. Connect your channels

Add website chat, sign in with Google or Microsoft for email, and connect WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger.

2. Point Magpie at your help content

Keep your Zendesk help center live while you move: Magpie can read it as a website, up to 50 pages, and read it again every day.

3. Test, then go live

Start in Draft mode, run a replay test on past questions, and switch to Auto when Magpie passes.
