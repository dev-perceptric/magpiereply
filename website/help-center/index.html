04 / Magpie

Every article makes Magpie smarter

Magpie reads your published articles every 15 minutes. Update a policy, and Magpie's answers change with it. Each reply names the articles it used.

  • Changed articles are read again on their own
  • Every reply lists the articles behind it
  • No article that answers the question? Magpie hands off to your team
