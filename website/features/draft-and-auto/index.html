02 / Auto

Auto, once Magpie has proved itself

A replay test answers up to 30 past conversations again and grades each one against what your team sent. Auto unlocks when it passes.

  • Auto needs a replay test of at least 10 replies
  • At least 90% must pass
  • Chosen for each inbox: Off, Draft or Auto
  • Refunds, flagged messages and handoffs still wait for a person

03 / Handoffs

A person, whenever it matters

Magpie hands the chat to your team when a customer asks for a person, is upset, needs a refund or asks something your sources don't cover.

  • A short note so nobody asks the customer twice
  • After two answers, a customer who still needs help goes to your team
  • Sent to the team you choose, like Support or Billing
  • The customer is told a teammate will reply
