01 / CONNECT

Sign in once. That's the whole setup.

Click Connect and the app's own sign-in page opens. Approve MagpieReply there, and you're back on your settings page with the app connected.

  • No API keys to find, copy or paste
  • Each team connects its own account
  • Keys are stored encrypted and refreshed before they expire
  • Disconnect any time from the same page

02 / READ-ONLY

Magpie looks. It never changes a thing.

Magpie only uses tools an app marks as read-only. Lookups about a customer use their verified email, filled in by code, so Magpie can't look up anyone else.

  • Tools that change data are never offered to Magpie
  • Lookups only for the signed-in customer, or one who proved it by email code
  • Issue links, ids and internal comments are never shared with customers
  • Every lookup shows up on the Traces page

03 / STRIPE

"Was I charged twice?" answered in seconds

Magpie checks the customer's recent charges in your own Stripe account and spots double payments. Refunds stay with your team.

  • Each charge explained with its date and amount
  • Double payments found and flagged
  • Refunds of $50 or more go to a teammate to review
  • Bank disputes go straight to Billing

04 / LINEAR

Tell customers when a bug is fixed

When a customer reports a problem, Magpie checks Linear for a matching issue and tells them its status in plain words.

  • Whether the bug is known, or already fixed
  • Searches only your own team's issues
  • No issue links, ids or teammate names shared
  • New problems go to your team with a note

Questions about apps

What can Magpie see in my apps?

Only what read-only tools return, and only for the customer in the conversation. In Stripe that's their recent charges. In Linear it's whether a matching issue exists and its status.

Can Magpie change anything?

No. Tools that create, update or delete are never offered to Magpie. Refunds and other changes stay with your team.

How do I disconnect an app?

Click Disconnect next to the app in Settings, AI agent, Apps. Magpie stops using it right away.

When are HubSpot and Shopify coming?

They're next. Their sign-in is built, and their lookups come once each can be locked to the customer who's asking, the same way Stripe is.
